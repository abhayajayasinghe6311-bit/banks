# SPA-W medical guideline review

Study Garden uses original, unofficial medical revision questions. It is not an authorised prescribing system.

A scheduled GitHub Action attempts to check seven official Australian medical guideline websites every Monday. Its first successful run records baseline visible-text fingerprints. When content changes later, it **opens a human clinical-review issue**. A page redesign may trigger an issue even when medical advice has not changed; some published guideline changes may not be detected. It is not a comprehensive systematic evidence review.

Reviewers must check clinical guidance and effective dates before any drug, investigation or answer-key correction is approved. **The monitoring workflow never edits bank JSON, clinical answers or browser progress.** Use local hospital protocols, current references, drug allergy, renal function, pregnancy status, age and antimicrobial resistance before real-life care.

Published training material may lag guidelines until a human reviewer corrects it. The workflow does not certify that all questions are up to date.
